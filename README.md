# Product Work

Product documents, in full rather than as a summary.

## [Campus Connect PRD](CAMPUS_CONNECT_PRD.pdf)

An AI concierge for campus life. A student runs their week across six or more apps (the LMS, email, WhatsApp, Instagram, the student portal) and loses deadlines and study partners in the gaps. Campus Connect pulls that into one place: a daily briefing that ranks what matters today, and study-partner matching on schedule, skills and goals.

25 pages, version 2.0. Written during the IIT Patna programme in Product Management and Agentic AI.

| Section | What's in it |
|---|---|
| Executive summary | The problem, the core hypothesis, the ask |
| User and persona | Primary persona and her day, before and after |
| MVP scope | What made the cut, what moved to phase 2, and why |
| User stories | Stories with acceptance criteria |
| Architecture | System overview and the reasoning behind each stack choice |
| Launch metrics | North Star, an L1–L3 metric tree, guardrail metrics, phase-gate reviews |

Market figures and targets in the document are planning assumptions, not shipped results.
